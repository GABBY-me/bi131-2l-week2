5. RESOURCE INTERPRETATION

The WSL2 environment has 3.7 GiB of total RAM and approximately 954 GB of available disk space. The /dev/dxg device is present, indicating that WSL2 has a GPU virtualization interface. However, nvidia-smi is not available, no NVIDIA device interface is shown under /dev, and the NVIDIA driver information is not present under /proc/driver/nvidia.

Therefore, a compatible NVIDIA GPU cannot be confirmed as visible or available for AlphaFold 3 inference in the current WSL2 environment.


6. ALPHAFOLD 3 REQUIREMENTS COMPARISON

The official AlphaFold 3 documentation states that GPU resources are required for the inference stage. The current environment does not expose a usable NVIDIA GPU. The environment also has only 3.7 GiB of RAM. Although approximately 954 GB of disk space is available, the lack of a visible NVIDIA GPU and limited RAM are major limitations for running AlphaFold 3 locally.

CONCLUSION

Full local AlphaFold 3 inference is not appropriate on this system. The current WSL2 environment does not provide a visible compatible NVIDIA GPU, and the available RAM is limited. The available disk space is substantial but does not overcome the GPU and memory limitations.
